# Name

**The Reliable Initiator**

---

# Core Identity Statement

*(Inspired by* Atomic Habits *by* **James Clear*)*

> **I am the kind of person people can rely on because I start important things promptly, even when I don’t feel like it.**

Short version:

> **I start. I’m reliable.**

---

# Who I Am Becoming

* Calm under pressure
* Confident through action
* Dependable without being intense
* Someone who trusts themselves to act

---

# My Core Value

**Reliability**

I protect my self-respect by following through on what I commit to.

---

# My Boundary (Non-Negotiable)

> **I am reliable to my commitments — not to every request.**

I choose first.
Then I show up fully.

---

# My Enemy (Name It)

**Hesitation disguised as comfort**

* “No energy”
* “I’ll do it later”
* Doomscrolling instead of starting

I do not trade self-respect for temporary relief.

---

# My Rule of Action

> **When something needs to be done, I start it promptly.**

Starting is success.
Finishing can come later.

---

# Identity Proof Habits

## 1. Immediate Start Rule

Start any important task for **2 minutes**.
No planning. No pressure.

## 2. Daily Reliability Signal

Do **one small act of follow-through** every day.

## 3. No-Relief Rule

Before distraction, ask:

> *“What am I avoiding starting?”*
> Touch it briefly first.

---

# Decision Filter

> **Would the reliable version of me start this now — or decline it clearly?**

Only two correct actions:

* Start
* Decline

Avoiding is not an option.

---

# Confidence Source

My confidence comes from **acting without hesitation**, not from results or motivation.

---

# Identity Mantra

> **I don’t wait to feel ready. I act, because that’s who I am.**

---

# Reminder

Every small start is a **vote for my identity**.
I am becoming reliable by proving it — quietly, daily.
