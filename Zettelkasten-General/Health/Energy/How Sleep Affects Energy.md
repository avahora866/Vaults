# Restores Brain Chemistry

* **Adenosine clearance**: Builds up during the day and causes sleepiness. Deep sleep clears it, resetting wakefulness.
* **Neurotransmitter replenishment**: Dopamine, norepinephrine, and serotonin systems reset during sleep. Poor sleep = low drive, focus, and mood.

# Supports Mitochondrial Function

* Sleep increases **mitochondrial efficiency** in muscles and neurons.
* Poor sleep = reduced ATP production = physical and mental fatigue.

# Hormonal Rebalancing

| Hormone              | Sleep Effect                       | Sleep Deprivation Effect                     |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Cortisol**         | Regulated by sleep timing          | Elevated baseline → chronic fatigue, stress  |
| **Growth hormone**   | Released during deep sleep         | Impaired recovery, muscle fatigue            |
| **Insulin**          | Sensitivity improves with sleep    | Resistance increases → energy crashes        |
| **Leptin & Ghrelin** | Balance hunger and satiety signals | Ghrelin up, leptin down = hunger, low energy |

# Cognitive Energy

* Sleep enhances **working memory**, **processing speed**, and **mental endurance**.
* REM sleep supports **creative thinking** and **emotional regulation**.
* Lack of sleep causes:

  * Poor decision-making
  * Decreased motivation
  * Lower reaction time

---

# Sleep Quality vs Quantity

| Factor                | Effect on Energy                             |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **7–9 hrs/night**     | Ideal for most adults                        |
| **Fragmented sleep**  | Less restorative, even if total time is high |
| **Late sleep onset**  | Misaligns circadian rhythm                   |
| **Sleep consistency** | Increases circadian and metabolic stability  |

---

# Types of Fatigue from Sleep Deprivation

| Fatigue Type     | Caused By                                    |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Physical**     | Poor deep sleep (non-REM stage 3)            |
| **Mental**       | Poor REM or fragmented sleep                 |
| **Emotional**    | Low REM → poor mood regulation, irritability |
| **Motivational** | Dopamine system disrupted → apathy           |

---

# Sleep Debt

* Accumulates over time. Even **1 hour of lost sleep per night** adds up.
* Can cause **persistent fatigue** even after 1–2 nights of good sleep.
* Needs **multiple consistent nights** of full sleep to recover.

---

# Summary

Sleep:

* Clears chemical fatigue (adenosine)
* Resets motivation and focus neurotransmitters
* Rebalances hormones controlling appetite and stress
* Directly affects both mental and physical energy systems

Poor sleep will **amplify every other cause of fatigue**, including poor diet, stress, and circadian misalignment.
