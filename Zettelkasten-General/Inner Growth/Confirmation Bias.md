Cognitive dissonance is the *pain* of inconsistency. Confirmation bias is the *painkiller* (or rather, the preventative vaccine).

People use confirmation bias to filter out conflicting information **before** it can cause dissonance. It works as a protective perimeter around your beliefs.

| Stage | What Happens | The Role of Confirmation Bias |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Exposure** | New information approaches your mind. | **Selective Exposure:** You actively ignore news sources or people that contradict you. You only consume data that agrees with you. |
| **2. Interpretation** | You see ambiguous data. | **Biased Assimilation:** You interpret neutral facts as proof you are right. (e.g., A market crash isn't "I was wrong," it's "Market manipulation"). |
| **3. Memory** | You recall the event later. | **Selective Recall:** You forget the times you were wrong and vividly remember the times you were right. |

**The Mechanism:**
If you let contradictory information in, you have to do the heavy mental lifting of restructuring your entire belief system (Cognitive Dissonance).
By using Confirmation Bias, you reject the data at the door. You save energy and keep your ego intact.
