# Decision-Making Under Uncertainty

Focus: Making decisions in situations with incomplete or uncertain information.
- [[Decision Trees]]: Visualize decisions, probabilities, and outcomes.
- [[Scenario Planning]]: Develops multiple plausible future scenarios to prepare for uncertainty.
- [[Expected Utility Theory]]: Quantifies decisions based on probabilities and potential outcomes. 

---
# Innovation and Creative Decision Frameworks

Focus: Generating and evaluating creative ideas or solutions.
- Design Thinking: Empathizes with users, defines problems, ideates, prototypes, and tests solutions.
- Brainstorming: Encourages idea generation without immediate judgment.
- [[TRIZ (Theory of Inventive Problem Solving)]]: Provides systematic tools for innovation.

---
# Process Improvement Frameworks
Focus: Streamlining and optimizing processes.
- [[Six Sigma DMAIC]]: Defines, measures, analyses, improves, and controls processes. 
- [[Lean Principles]]: Reduces waste and improves efficiency.
- [[PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act)]]: A cyclical framework for continuous improvement. 
--- 
# Behavioural and Psychological Decision Frameworks

Focus: Understanding human biases and improving decision quality.
- Prospect Theory #NOTES : Examines how people perceive gains and losses.
- Nudge Theory #NOTES : Encourages positive behaviours without restricting choices.
- [[Cognitive Bias Mitigation Frameworks]]: Address biases like anchoring or confirmation bias. 
---
# Problem-Solving and Root Cause Analysis Frameworks

Focus: Identifying the root causes of problems and finding effective solutions.
 - [[Ishikawa's Diagram]] (Fishbone Diagram): Analyses the root causes of a problem.
 - 5 Whys Analysis: Sequentially asks "why" to dig deeper into the root cause
 - [[Gap Analysis]]: Identifies the difference between the current state and the desired state, along with causes.

---
# Strategic Decision Frameworks

Focus: Long-term planning and strategy formulation.
- [[SWOT]] Evaluates strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
- [[PEST]]: Assesses macro-environmental factors (Political, Economic, Social, Technological).
- Porter’s Five Forces: Analyses industry competitiveness and market forces.
- Balanced Scorecard: Links strategic objectives to performance metrics.

---
# Prioritization and Resource Allocation Frameworks

Focus: Ranking options or allocating limited resources efficiently.
- [[Pareto Analysis]]: Focuses on the vital few factors contributing most to the outcome (80/20 rule).
- Eisenhower Matrix: Categorizes tasks into urgent/important quadrants for prioritization.
- [[Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP)]]: Uses pairwise comparisons to rank alternatives and allocate resources.
- MoSCoW Method: Prioritizes features or requirements as Must Have, Should Have, Could Have, and Won’t Have.
- [[K-T Matrix]]: It is designed to help systematically evaluate and choose the best course of action when faced with multiple options and criteria,

| **Framework**                           | **Purpose**                                                                     | **Key Components**                                                    | **Use Cases**                                                   | **Strengths**                                                       | **Limitations**                                                       |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Decision Trees**                      | Visualize and evaluate decisions based on outcomes and probabilities.           | Nodes, branches, outcomes, probabilities.                             | Risk assessment, investment decisions, operational planning.    | Simple to understand, helps with structured decision-making.        | May not capture complex dependencies, time-consuming for large trees. |
| **Scenario Planning**                   | Explore future possibilities and uncertainties to prepare for change.           | Scenarios, assumptions, potential events.                             | Long-term strategic planning, risk management, forecasting.     | Provides strategic flexibility, useful in uncertain environments.   | Can be time-consuming, dependent on quality of assumptions.           |
| **Expected Utility Theory**             | Evaluate choices by comparing expected outcomes based on utility values.        | Utility function, probability, outcome.                               | Decision-making under uncertainty, economics, finance.          | Provides a quantitative basis for decision-making.                  | May oversimplify preferences, requires accurate utility assessments.  |
| **TRIZ**                                | Solve inventive problems by resolving contradictions in system designs.         | 40 inventive principles, contradiction matrix, laws of evolution.     | Engineering design, product innovation, problem-solving.        | Systematic approach to innovation, applies across industries.       | Can be complex for beginners, requires deep understanding.            |
| **Six Sigma DMAIC**                     | Improve process quality through structured steps.                               | Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control.                           | Process improvement, manufacturing, quality control.            | Data-driven, emphasizes process control and continuous improvement. | Resource-intensive, may be too rigid for some contexts.               |
| **Lean Principles**                     | Eliminate waste and optimize efficiency in processes.                           | Value stream mapping, continuous improvement, waste reduction.        | Manufacturing, service industries, operational optimization.    | Focuses on efficiency and value creation.                           | May overlook innovation, can lead to burnout without balance.         |
| **PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act)**            | Improve processes through iterative cycles.                                     | Plan, Do, Check, Act.                                                 | Continuous improvement, quality management, project management. | Simple, iterative, emphasizes constant improvement.                 | May become repetitive without clear objectives or flexibility.        |
| **Cognitive Bias Mitigation Framework** | Reduce the impact of cognitive biases on decision-making.                       | Debiasing techniques, structured decision-making, perspective-taking. | Decision-making, risk analysis, strategic planning.             | Promotes rational thinking, minimizes errors from biases.           | Time-consuming to implement, requires continual practice.             |
| **Ishikawa Diagram**                    | Identify root causes of a problem through a visual tool.                        | Fishbone diagram, categories of potential causes.                     | Root cause analysis, quality management, process improvement.   | Simple to use, effective for identifying complex causes.            | May oversimplify issues, does not prioritize causes.                  |
| **Gap Analysis**                        | Identify differences between current and desired performance levels.            | Current state, desired state, action plan.                            | Strategic planning, performance evaluation, project management. | Helps identify areas for improvement, actionable steps.             | May overlook underlying causes, assumes data accuracy.                |
| **SWOT**                                | Assess internal and external factors affecting a situation.                     | Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats.                        | Business strategy, market analysis, project planning.           | Simple to use, helps visualize strategic factors.                   | Can be oversimplified, static analysis.                               |
| **PEST**                                | Analyze external macro-environmental factors influencing an organization.       | Political, Economic, Social, Technological factors.                   | Market analysis, strategic planning, environmental scanning.    | Provides a broad view of external factors, easy to implement.       | May miss specific issues, requires up-to-date information.            |
| **Pareto Analysis**                     | Identify the most significant factors impacting a problem or goal.              | 80/20 rule, frequency of causes or effects.                           | Prioritization, resource allocation, process improvement.       | Simple, highlights the most impactful factors.                      | May overlook smaller, but still important, factors.                   |
| **AHP (Analytic Hierarchy Process)**    | Evaluate complex decisions by structuring criteria in a hierarchy.              | Hierarchical structure, pairwise comparisons, weighting of factors.   | Complex decision-making, project evaluation, prioritization.    | Provides a structured, quantitative decision-making approach.       | Requires expert judgment, can be time-consuming.                      |
| **K-T Matrix**                          | Prioritize requirements and determine design solutions based on customer needs. | Customer requirements, technical requirements, importance ratings.    | Product development, customer feedback analysis.                | Helps prioritize customer needs and solutions.                      | May be subjective, requires accurate data.                            |
