# Sedan
- A traditional, four-door vehicle with a separate trunk. Known for comfort, fuel efficiency, and practicality.
# Coupe
- A two-door car, often with a sporty design and a sloping roofline. Typically offers a more dynamic driving experience, with limited rear-seat space.
# Hatchback
- A compact car with a rear door that swings upwards, offering more versatile cargo space. It combines the practicality of a sedan with some SUV-like flexibility.
# SUV (Sport Utility Vehicle)
- A larger vehicle designed for both on-road and light off-road driving, often with higher ground clearance and available all-wheel or four-wheel drive.
# Crossover
- A type of SUV built on a car's platform (as opposed to a truck's platform), offering similar space and features but with more comfort and better fuel efficiency.
# 4x4 (Four-Wheel Drive)
- A vehicle designed to distribute power to all four wheels, enhancing traction and off-road capability. These are popular for rugged terrains, off-roading, and heavy towing.
# Pickup Truck
- A vehicle with a separate cabin and an open cargo area (bed). They are often used for hauling and towing, with varying cab configurations (regular, extended, or crew).
#Electric Vehicle (EV)
- A car powered entirely by electricity, with no internal combustion engine. EVs are known for their zero emissions and lower maintenance requirements.
# Hybrid Car
- A vehicle that combines a gasoline engine with an electric motor to improve fuel efficiency and reduce emissions. They typically operate on both power sources.
