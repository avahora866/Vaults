The key is to learn to focus better visually if you want to bring about higher levels of cognitive or mental focus

- 40 seconds: Focus for 40 seconds on a spot where you want your focus to be. This helps release chemicals that increase focus
- Closing the eyes: Closing the eyes is one of the best ways of creating a cone of auditory attention. When learning by listening close your eyes as keeping them open will distract you with other items.
- Agitation: You will feel some agitation which is good because you are doing it right
- Cone of attention: It is easy to focus on phone because:
  - it is a limited size so a cone of attention is created when you look at it
  - Any time we are looking at things that have motion our attention system will naturally gravitate towards them
      - The above 2 tips can be used to explain why it is easy to read on phone and harder to read on paper
- Length of Focus

  - 5 to 10 min of warm up (not fully focused)
