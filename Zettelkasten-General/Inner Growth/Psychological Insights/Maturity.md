Being mature can be split into two sections:

1. Looking Mature
2. Doing your responsibilities

# Looking mature

This is making sure you are properly groomed (nails, beard, hair, smell). Also making sure your clothes fit you otherwise you look too tight or too loose (lazy).

# Responsibilities

When something is important (doesn't have to eb important to you) then accomplishing the task with no whining or whinging in a sufficient amount of time would be being mature
