The scarcity loop refers to a cycle of behaviour that can compel people in a near-addictive manner. It has three key components:
- opportunity
- unpredictable rewards
- quick repeatability

First, there needs to be an opportunity to obtain something valuable – something that could improve one’s life, like money or status.

Second, the rewards offered must be delivered unpredictably, creating a sense of anticipation and suspense that pulls us in. 

Finally, there must be the ability to quickly repeat the behaviour – this greatly strengthens the effect.
