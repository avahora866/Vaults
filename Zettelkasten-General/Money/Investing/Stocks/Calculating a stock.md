# Ways to Estimate Stock Movement

Here are some common methods used to predict stock direction:

 Technical Analysis (Chart Patterns & Indicators) #LINK
    - Moving Averages (e.g., 50-day & 200-day)
    - Relative Strength Index (RSI)
    - MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
    - Support & Resistance levels
 Fundamental Analysis (Company Valuation & Earnings) #LINK
    - Earnings per Share (EPS) Growth 
    - Price-to-Earnings Ratio (P/E)
    - Revenue & Profit Trends
    - Industry & Economic Conditions 
3. **Sentiment & News Analysis**
    - Analyst Ratings
    - Economic Reports
    - Social Media & News Sentiment
 Machine Learning & AI Predictions #NOTES 
    - Uses historical data to detect patterns
    - Not 100% accurate, but can improve decision-making
