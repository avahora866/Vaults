# Trend-Following Indicators

## Moving Averages (SMA/EMA)
* Show the overall direction of the market by smoothing price data.
* Used in strategies like **Moving Average Crossover** (short MA crosses long MA \= entry/exit signals).

## MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
- Combines trend-following and momentum.
* Consists of the **MACD line**, **Signal line**, and **Histogram**.
* Signals:
	- **MACD line crossing above the Signal line** \= Bullish
	- *MACD line crossing below the Signal line** \= Bearish
	- Histogram showing momentum strength

# Momentum Indicators

## Stochastic Oscillator
* Compares closing price to a price range over a set period.
* Ranges from 0 to 100\.
	- *Above 80** \= Overbought
	- *Below 20** \= Oversold
* Good for identifying potential reversals.

## CCI (Commodity Channel Index)
* Measures the difference between the current price and its historical average.
* Over \+100 \= Overbought, below \-100 \= Oversold

---

# Volatility Indicators

## Bollinger Bands
* Bands around a moving average using standard deviations.
* **Price touching the upper band**: Overbought
* **Price touching the lower band**: Oversold
* Can indicate potential breakouts when bands tighten ("squeeze").

## ATR (Average True Range)
* Measures market volatility.
* Larger ATR \= more volatile market
* Useful for setting stop-loss levels and sizing positions

---

# Volume-Based Indicators

## On-Balance Volume (OBV)
* Adds volume on up days, subtracts on down days.
* Rising OBV \= volume supports uptrend
* Falling OBV \= volume supports downtrend

## Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP)
* Average price a security has traded at throughout the day, based on volume and price.
* Often used by institutional traders to assess fair value.

---

# Other Noteworthy Signals

## Fibonacci Retracement
* Identifies potential support/resistance levels based on percentage retracements (23.6%, 38.2%, 61.8%).
* Often used after strong price moves.
## Pivot Points
* Used to determine overall market trends over different time frames.
* Include support and resistance levels based on previous highs/lows/close.

## Ichimoku Cloud
* A complex indicator showing support, resistance, momentum, and trend.
* Includes five lines and a cloud that provides a quick visual of market sentiment.

---

# Combining Indicators
Most traders **combine multiple indicators** to confirm signals and reduce false positives. For example:
* **RSI \+ MACD**: Confirms momentum and trend reversal.
* **ADX \+ Moving Averages**: Confirm trend strength and direction.
* **Bollinger Bands \+ RSI**: Identify volatility breakouts with overbought/oversold context.
