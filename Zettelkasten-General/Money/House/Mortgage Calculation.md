Mortgage lenders don't typically factor in regional cost-of-living differences like commuting or higher rent when calculating how much you can borrow. So if you earn £50k in London or £50k outside London, **your potential mortgage offer could be roughly the same** in principle.

However, here are some nuances:

- **Affordability checks**: Lenders look at your outgoings — including existing debts, dependents, and regular expenses. If you’re paying a lot for commuting or rent in London, it might reduce the amount they’re willing to lend because your disposable income is lower.
- **Lifestyle costs** aren’t automatically adjusted based on location, but if your bank statements show consistently high travel or living costs, that can impact the affordability assessment.
- **Different lenders have different approaches**. Some use more detailed affordability models that might indirectly reflect location-based spending. Others use simple income multiples (e.g. 4.5x your salary) as a baseline.

So: same salary, same headline mortgage amount — but your actual approved amount might vary depending on your spending patterns, especially if you're living and commuting in a high-cost area like London.
