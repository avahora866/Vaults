# Ruqsati (رخصتی):
This is a cultural practice (not a religious requirement). It refers to when the bride formally leaves her parents’ home to go live with the groom. This usually happens after the wedding events, often on the same day or soon after the **walimah**. It marks the start of the married couple’s domestic life.

# Nikah (نكاح):
This is the Islamic marriage contract. It's a **religious and legal** agreement between the bride and groom. It includes:

  * Proposal and acceptance (Ijab and Qubool)
  * Presence of two adult Muslim witnesses
  * Agreement on **mahr** (mandatory gift from groom to bride)
  * Consent of the **wali** (guardian) for the bride (especially in traditional jurisprudence)

  After Nikah is done, the couple is Islamically considered husband and wife. **Sexual relations become halal** (permissible) from this point forward. However, cultural norms often delay consummation until **ruqsati**.

# Walimah (وليمة):
 This is the marriage banquet hosted by the groom’s side. It’s a **Sunnah** (Prophetic tradition) and is done to announce the marriage publicly. It usually takes place:

  * After the Nikah
  * Often after or around the time of Ruqsati
  * Sometimes delayed, depending on cultural customs

### Consummation of Marriage

* **In Islam**, consummation (sexual relations) is allowed immediately **after Nikah**.
* **In practice**, many cultures (like South Asian ones) wait until after **Ruqsati**. The idea is that the bride and groom don't start living together until then.
* **Walimah** is recommended to be held **after consummation** of the marriage, but this is not a strict rule.
