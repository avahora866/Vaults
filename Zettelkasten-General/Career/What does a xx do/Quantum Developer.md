# Main Purpose
- Design and implement quantum algorithms
- Bridge theory and hardware to solve problems classical systems struggle with
- Enable practical use of quantum computing in business and research

# Day-to-Day Tasks
1. Develop quantum algorithms and circuits using SDKs like Qiskit, Cirq
2. Integrate quantum and classical code (hybrid workflows)
3. Simulate and test circuits for noise resilience and optimization
4. Collaborate with physicists, engineers, and domain experts
5. Read research papers, explore emerging quantum algorithms
6. Write or contribute to libraries, APIs, and documentation
7. Run workloads on quantum hardware or cloud simulators

# Role by Company Type
1. **Startups**
    - Build quantum prototypes for niche domains
    - License early-stage tech or win grant funding
    - Devs focus on rapid prototyping, hybrid models, and demos
2. **Big Tech (IBM, Google, AWS)**
    - Monetize cloud quantum platforms and tools
    - Devs work on SDKs, hardware integration, and infrastructure
3. **Research Labs**
    - Advance foundational quantum theory and hardware
    - Funded via grants; devs write research code and publish
4. **Enterprise Users (Banks, Pharma)**
    - Use quantum tools to gain early advantage
    - Devs model business problems and build internal tooling
