Two numbers are **coprime** (or **relatively prime**) if their greatest common divisor (gcd) is 1. In other words, they have no common positive factors other than 1.

### Key Points

- Coprime numbers do not have to be prime themselves. They simply shouldn't share any prime factors.
- The numbers do not need to be consecutive.
- If gcd(a, b) = 1, then a and b are coprime.

### Examples

1. **8 and 15**
    - Factors of 8: 1, 2, 4, 8
    - Factors of 15: 1, 3, 5, 15
    - Common factor: 1
    - gcd(8, 15) = 1, so 8 and 15 are coprime.
2. **14 and 25**
    - Factors of 14: 1, 2, 7, 14
    - Factors of 25: 1, 5, 25
    - Common factor: 1
