    - Summary: Understanding numbers and basic calculations.
2. [[Algebra MOC]]: Manipulating equations, expressions, and solving for variables.
    - Summary: The study of mathematical symbols and rules for manipulating them.

### **Core Topics**

1. **Linear Algebra**
    - Prerequisite: Algebra
    - Summary: Study of vectors, matrices, and linear transformations, used in modeling systems and financial calculations.
    - Matrix operations and transformations (essential for quantum mechanics
    - Basics of vector addition and scalar quantities
    - eigenvalues
2. **Probability and Statistics**
    - Prerequisite: Arithmetic and Algebra
    - Summary: Tools for analyzing uncertainty, risk, and trends, foundational for financial modeling and analysis.
    - Distributions (normal, binomial, Poisson)
	  * Statistical inference (hypothesis testing, confidence intervals)
	  * Bayesian methods
	  * Monte Carlo simulations
3. **Calculus (Differential and Integral)**
    - Prerequisite: Algebra and Arithmetic
    - Summary: Techniques for modeling change and accumulation, essential for pricing derivatives and optimizing systems.
    - Derivatives (rate of change, motion equations).
    - Integrals (area under curves, total work done).
    - multivariable calculas
    - Differentiation, integration, optimization (e.g., gradient descent)
4. **Discrete Mathematics**
    - Prerequisite: Algebra
    - Summary: Study of mathematical structures like graphs, sets, and logic, used in algorithms and computational systems.
5. **Numerical Methods**
    - Prerequisite: Linear Algebra and Calculus
    - Summary: Techniques for approximating mathematical computations, crucial for financial simulations.
      * Root finding, numerical optimization, matrix decomposition
6. **Optimization**
    - Prerequisite: Calculus and Linear Algebra
    - Summary: Finding the best solution under given constraints, vital for portfolio optimization and resource allocation.
7. **Time Series Analysis**
    - Prerequisite: Probability and Statistics
    - Summary: Analysing sequential data points over time, used in stock market and financial forecasting.
8. **Stochastic Processes**
    - Prerequisite: Probability and Calculus
    - Summary: Study of systems that evolve over time with inherent randomness, important for pricing financial derivatives.
9. **Game Theory**
    - Prerequisite: Algebra and Probability
    - Summary: Mathematical study of strategic interactions and decision-making.
10. **Financial Mathematics**
    - Prerequisite: Calculus, Probability, and Linear Algebra
    - Summary: Application of mathematical techniques to solve problems in finance, such as pricing options and managing risk.
    -  Time value of money
	  * Options pricing (Black-Scholes, Greeks)
	  * VaR
	  * Time series analysis
