These are triangles that have the same shape but may have diffrent sizes.
This means that the:
- triangles have the same angles
- their sides are the same proportion (two sides have the same ration of length e.g if one side is half the size in the smaller triangle the other side follow the same ratio)

# Real work example

Imagine you want to find the height of a tree without climbing it. You can use the concept of similar triangles by following these steps:

1. Place a stick vertically in the ground that is 1 meter tall.
2. Measure the length of the shadow the stick casts. Let's say the shadow is 2 meters long.
3. Measure the length of the shadow cast by the tree. Suppose the tree's shadow is 10 meters long.

Since the sun's rays are parallel, the triangles formed by the stick and its shadow and by the tree and its shadow are similar. This means their corresponding angles are equal, and their corresponding sides are proportional.

We can set up the proportion using the property of similar triangles:

$$
\frac{Height\;Of\;Stick}{Length\;Of\;Sticks \;Shadow} = \frac{Height\;Of\;Tree}{Length\;Of\;Trees\;Shadow}
$$


$$
\frac{1m}{2m}= \frac{Height Of Tree}{10 m}
$$

[[Cross-Multiplying]] gives:
$$
Height\;Of\;Tree\;= \frac{1m}{2m}\;*\;10m\;= \; 5m
$$

Therefore, the tree is 5 meters tall.

This method works because the triangles are similar, so the ratios of corresponding sides are equal, allowing us to solve for the unknown height.
