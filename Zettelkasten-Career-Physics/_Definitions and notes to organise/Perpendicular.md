**Perpendicular** means that two lines, planes, or vectors intersect at a **right angle** (90 degrees).

### Key Points:

* **Right Angle**: Two objects are perpendicular if they form an angle of exactly 90° between them.
* **In 2D**: Two lines are perpendicular if the angle between them is 90°.
* **In 3D**: Two vectors are perpendicular if the dot product between them is zero, which indicates a 90° angle.

### Visualizing Perpendicularity:

* Imagine the **floor** and **walls** of a room. The floor is perpendicular to the walls because they meet at a 90° angle.
* If you have a **pen** and place it flat on a table (the surface), the pen is perpendicular to the surface of the table if it forms a 90° angle with the table's surface.

### Mathematical Definition for Vectors:

For two vectors $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$ to be perpendicular, their **dot product** must be zero:

$$
\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = 0
$$

### Why It's Important:

* **In geometry**: Perpendicular lines help define **right angles**, which are essential for constructing shapes like squares and rectangles.
* **In physics**: Perpendicularity is used to describe forces (e.g., when a force is applied at a right angle to a surface).
