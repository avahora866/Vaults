This is the central hub for physics self-study, structured as a TRUNK/BRANCH/LEAF Zettelkasten. Each subject below has its own MOC (Map of Content) which links to that subject's TRUNK note, and from there out to its BRANCH and LEAF notes.

The aim of this vault is a solid *conceptual* grounding across physics broad enough to later expand into engineering, robotics, and other applied fields — not exhaustive textbook coverage of any single branch.

### Suggested Learning Path
1. **[[01 Mechanics Map of Content|Mechanics]]** and **[[01 Waves and Oscillations Map of Content|Waves]]** — the foundations. Nearly everything downstream (fields, thermodynamics, quantum mechanics) reuses the language of forces, energy, and oscillation built here.
2. **[[01 Electromagnetism Map of Content|Electromagnetism]]** and **[[01 Thermodynamics Map of Content|Thermodynamics]]** — the two great 19th-century field theories, and the two subjects an engineer or roboticist will touch most often outside pure mechanics.
3. **[[01 Modern Physics Map of Content|Modern Physics]]** — where classical intuition starts to break: light as a particle, particles as waves, energy in discrete steps, relativity of time and space.
4. **[[01 Quantum Mechanics Foundations Map of Content|Quantum Mechanics Foundations]]** — the mathematical and conceptual core of quantum theory (states, measurement, the Schrödinger equation), building directly on the wave and oscillation ideas from step 1 and the quantization ideas from step 3.

### How the branches connect
* **Harmonic Motion** (Mechanics) → **Quantum Harmonic Oscillator** (Quantum Mechanics Foundations): the same equation, two regimes.
* **Waves and Superposition** (Waves) → **Quantum Wavefunctions and Superposition** (Quantum Mechanics Foundations): quantum states obey the same superposition math as classical waves.
* **Energy Quantization** (Modern Physics) → **Schrödinger Equation and State Evolution** (Quantum Mechanics Foundations): quantized atomic energy levels are a direct output of solving the Schrödinger equation.
* **Electromagnetic Waves** (Electromagnetism) → **Wave-Particle Duality** (Modern Physics): light's dual nature is where the classical and quantum pictures collide.
