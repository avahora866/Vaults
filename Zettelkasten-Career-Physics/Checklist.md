### How These Connect to Quantum Mechanics:

- **Harmonic Motion**: Quantum harmonic oscillators.
- **Waves and Superposition**: Quantum wavefunctions and superposition principles.
- **Energy Levels**: Understanding quantization in systems like atoms.
- **Electromagnetic Waves**: Photon behavior and polarization.

### Suggested Learning Path:

1. Start with **mechanics** and **waves** (these are foundational).
2. Move to **electricity and magnetism**, particularly if you're interested in quantum hardware.
3. Learn the basics of **modern physics** to bridge the gap to quantum concepts
