# Phase I: The Gesture (The Energy)

**Goal:** Capture the movement and intent. If this stage is stiff, the final image will be stiff, no matter how much you polish it.

1. **The Line of Action (LOA):**
    - Draw a single, sweeping line representing the spine.
    - **Check:** Is it a "C" curve or an "S" curve? (Avoid straight lines).
    - **Flow:** Ensure the line connects the head to the weight-bearing foot.
2. **The Bean (2D Mass):**
    - Draw two oval shapes for the Ribcage and Pelvis along the LOA.
    - **Check:** Identify the **Pinch** (compression side) and the **Stretch** (tension side).
    - **Tilt:** Ensure the masses are tilting against each other, not stacked perfectly vertical like a snowman.

---

# Phase II: The Construction (The Structure)

**Goal:** Solidify the gesture into 3D space. This solves your "structure looks wrong" issue.

3. **Mannequinization (Robo-Bean):**
    - Convert the round "Bean" ovals into box-like forms with corners.
    - **Orientation:** clearly define the Front, Side, and Bottom/Top planes.
    - **The Center Line:** Draw the line down the center of the chest and pelvis.
    - **Wrapping Lines:** Add "belt" and "collar" lines.
        - _If looking UP:_ Curve them upward (Rainbow).
        - _If looking DOWN:_ Curve them downward (Smiley).
4. **Limb Attachment:**
    - **Shoulder Girdle:** Draw the "Coat Hanger" (clavicles) floating on top of the ribcage. Pivot them up if the arms are raised.
    - **Hip Connection:** Locate the Great Trochanter (wider and lower than the waist).
    - **Cylinders:** Draw the limbs as cylinders. Use **T-Junctions** to show which part of the cylinder is in front (Foreshortening).

---

# Phase III: The Anatomy (The Design)

**Goal:** Apply biological logic to the mannequin.

5. **The Organic Layer:**
    - **Asymmetry:** Check every limb. Ensure you have a **Curve** (muscle) opposing a **Straight** (bone/tension).
        - _Check:_ Is the "thumb side" of the forearm higher/rounder than the pinky side?
        - _Check:_ Is the calf curve offset from the shin straight?
    - **The Ties:**
        - **Pec/Deltoid:** Ensure the Deltoid (shoulder) overlaps _on top_ of the Pectoral (chest).
        - **Knees:** Ensure the legs taper inward ("V" shape) from the hips to the center of gravity.

---

# Phase IV: The Line & Value (The Rendering)

**Goal:** Define light, gravity, and material.

6. **Inking (Line Hierarchy):**
    - **Ghosting:** Practice the stroke, then commit. Glide with your elbow/shoulder, lock your wrist.
    - **Weighting:** Thicken lines on the **Shadow Side** and at **Occlusion points** (armpits, chin, crotch). Thin the lines on the **Light Side**.
7. **Shadow Mapping:**
    - **Terminator:** Draw the specific shape of the shadow.
    - **Check:** Is the edge Hard (Cast Shadow) or Soft (Form Shadow)?
    - **Occlusion (AO):** Mark the deepest cracks (contacts, folds) with near-black.
8. **Color & Light:**
    - **Hue Shift:** When picking shadow colors, shift the hue (Warm light $\rightarrow$ Cool shadow). Do not just add black.
    - **Core Shadow:** Darken the band _just after_ the terminator.
    - **Reflected Light:** Lighten the shadow edge slightly (but keep it darker than the lit side).
    - **Subsurface Scattering:** Add a saturated Red/Orange line at the shadow edge on skin (especially ears/fingers).

---

# The "Stiffness" Debugger

_If you finish a drawing and it still looks stiff, ask these three questions:_

1. **"Did I draw a parallel tube?"** (Check for Asymmetry/Straight vs Curve).
2. **"Is the torso a rectangle?"** (Check for the Pinch/Stretch of the Bean).
3. **"Are the lines hairy?"** (Check for Ghosting and flow).
