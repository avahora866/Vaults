# 2. Bony Landmarks
*These are crucial reference points for measurement and construction because they are close to the skin's surface and are not obscured by muscle.*

- ***Shoulder:***
    - ***Acromion Process:** The bony tip of the shoulder.*
    - ***Clavicle (Collarbone):** The S-shaped bone at the top of the chest.*
- ***Elbow:***
    - ***Olecranon Process of Ulna:** The sharp point of the elbow.*
    - ***Medial and Lateral Epicondyles of Humerus:** The two bony bumps on either side of the elbow.*
- ***Wrist:***
    - ***Styloid Process of Ulna/Radius:** The prominent bumps on either side of thewrist.*

# 3. Major Muscle Groups

## Upper Arm
- ***Anterior (Flexors):***
    - *`[[Biceps Brachii]]`: Creates the main bulge on the front of the upper arm.*
- ***Posterior (Extensors):***
    - *`[[Triceps Brachii]]`: Defines the back of the upper arm.*

## Forearm
*The forearm is complex. Start by grouping the muscles.*
- ***Extensor Group (Top of forearm):** Pulls the hand and fingers back.*
- ***Flexor Group (Underside of forearm):** Curls the hand and fingers.*
- ***Brachioradialis:** A key muscle that wraps from the outer elbow down towards the thumb side of the wrist. Defines the top plane of the forearm.*
