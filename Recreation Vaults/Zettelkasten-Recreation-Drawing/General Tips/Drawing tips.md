# Sketch
## Process of sketching
When drawing anything separate your sketch into 3 sections 
### Messy Sketch
Initial messy sketch to get basic structure and proportions down= red
If drawing a figure or portrait, Details like eyes, mouths and etc should be left blanked or just blocked in
### Details
More details sketch but still not final to clean up structure as well as add the details = blue
should not be messy at all 
If drawing a figure/portrait here is where you add in eyes, ears and mouths

### Line Art
Clean line art = black
This is to easily distinguish between details and structure. Final line art is to get the weight of line proper
