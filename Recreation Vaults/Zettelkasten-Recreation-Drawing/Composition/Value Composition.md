## **6. Value Composition & Focal Point**

Values should guide the viewer’s eye through the artwork.

✅ **Use Contrast to Emphasize the Focal Point:**

- Brightest highlights and darkest shadows should be near the subject’s focus (e.g., face, hands).
- Low-contrast areas can be used to avoid distracting from the main subject.

✅ **Grouping Values for Readability:**

- Simplify value areas to avoid clutter.
- Good compositions have **light, midtone, and shadow masses** arranged clearly.
