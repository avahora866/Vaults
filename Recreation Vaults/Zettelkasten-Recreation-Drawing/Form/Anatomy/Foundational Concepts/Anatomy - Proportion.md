Body proportion studies the relationship of the parts of the human body to each other and to the whole
### Canons of Proportion

A "canon of proportions" is a set of rules for the proportions of the human body. These systems provide a framework for artists.
- **Ancient Egypt**: The Egyptians used a grid system to maintain consistent proportions in their art. The height of a figure was typically divided into 18 units, with key anatomical landmarks placed on specific grid lines. The basic unit of measurement was the fist.
- **Ancient Greece**: Greek sculptors like Polykleitos sought to create ideal human forms based on mathematical ratios. His _Canon_ described a system where the body's parts were proportionally related to create a harmonious whole.
- **Renaissance**: Leonardo da Vinci's _Vitruvian Man_ is a famous study of human proportions based on the writings of the Roman architect Vitruvius. It illustrates how the human body can be inscribed within both a circle and a square, symbolizing the connection between humanity and the universe.

### The Head as a Unit of Measurement

A common method used in figure drawing is to use the height of the head as a basic unit of measurement.
- **Average Adult**: A typical adult is about **7.5 heads tall**.
- **Idealized Figure**: In art, an idealized or "perfect" figure is often depicted as **8 heads tall**. This proportion gives the figure a sense of grace and nobility.
- **Heroic Figure**: For gods or superheroes, artists may use a proportion of **8.5 or even 9 heads tall** to create a more imposing and powerful look.

This 8-head system provides a set of useful landmarks:
- **0**: Top of the head
- **1**: Chin
- **2**: Nipples
- **3**: Navel
- **4**: Crotch (this is the halfway point of the body)
- **5**: Mid-thigh
- **6**: Bottom of the knees
- **7**: Mid-shin
- **8**: Soles of the feet

Other key proportions in this system include:
- The **shoulders** are approximately three head-widths across for a male and two and a half for a female.
- The **elbows** align with the navel.
- The **wrists** align with the crotch.
- The **fingertips** reach the mid-thigh.
- The **arm span** from fingertip to fingertip is roughly equal to the person's height.
### Male and Female Proportions

There are general proportional differences between adult males and females.

|Feature|Male|Female|
|---|---|---|
|**Shoulders**|Wider, typically 3 head-widths|Narrower, typically 2.5 head-widths|
|**Hips**|Narrower|Wider|
|**Waist**|Less defined|More defined and higher|
|**Center of Gravity**|Shoulders|Hips|
