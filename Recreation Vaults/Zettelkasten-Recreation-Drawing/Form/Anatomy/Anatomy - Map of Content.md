This is the central hub for all notes related to human anatomy for drawing. The goal is not to memorize medical terminology, but to understand the form, structure, and mechanics of the body to create believable figures.

---

### How to Use This MOC
1.  **Start with the Foundations.** Understand the core concepts of proportion and structure first.
2.  **Study Region by Region.** Use the Regional Breakdown section to focus your studies on one area at a time.
3.  **Create New Notes.** As you study a topic (e.g., the skull), create a new note for it. Then, link to that new note from this MOC.
