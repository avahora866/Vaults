# Female figure
Height: 7 1/2 heads tall

--------------------------------------------------------
Shoulders: 1 1/2 heads wide
Hips: 1 1/2 to 2 heads wide
Torso (includes pelvis): 3 heads tall

--------------------------------------------------------
Arm: 2 1/2 heads long
	Shoulder to elbow 1 and half head
	Elbow to wrist: 1 head
Hand: 1 head

--------------------------------------------------------
Leg: 3 heads
	Upper leg 1 and a half heads
	Lower leg 1 and a half heads
Feet: 1/2 head
# Male figure
Body = 7 to 8 x head.

--------------------------------------------------------
Neck = 1 x hand.
Trunk = 3 x head.

--------------------------------------------------------
Arm = 3.5 x head. 
Shoulders = 2 x head.
Hand = 1 x head (or a little less).

--------------------------------------------------------
Leg = 4 x head. 
Foot = 1 x forearm = ½ arm.
