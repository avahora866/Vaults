# Recipe Metadata
- **Name:** Teriyaki Sauce
- **Source:** [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/teriyaki-sauce](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/teriyaki-sauce)
- **Cuisine:** Japanese
- **Core Flavor Profile:** A rich, glossy glaze that is primarily savory (umami), sweet, and aromatic.

# Ingredient Analysis

|Ingredient|Role / Function|Flavor Contribution|
|---|---|---|
|Water|Solvent, Volume|Neutral base, dilutes the other ingredients.|
|Light Brown Soft Sugar|Sweetener, Thickener|Provides primary sweetness, molasses notes, and viscosity.|
|Light Soy Sauce|Flavor Base, Salinity|Primary source of salinity and `[[Umami]]`.|
|Garlic (crushed)|Aromatic|Adds a pungent, savory, and sharp aromatic quality.|
|Ginger (grated)|Aromatic|Adds a warm, zesty, and spicy aromatic note.|
|Cornflour|Thickening Agent|Creates a `[[Slurry]]` to give the sauce a thick, glossy, clinging texture.|
|Rice Wine Vinegar|Acidity, Balance|Adds a final sharp, acidic note to cut richness and balance the sweetness.|
|Sesame Seeds (optional)|Garnish, Texture|Nutty, roasted flavor and a slight crunch.|
|Spring Onions (optional)|Garnish, Aromatic|Fresh, sharp, mild onion flavor as a contrast.|

# Process Analysis

- **Step 1-2: Combine Base Ingredients & Simmer**
    - **Technique:** `[[Dissolving]]` and `[[Simmering]]`.
    - **Purpose:** To dissolve the sugar completely into the liquids and to infuse the aromatic flavors of the garlic and ginger into the sauce base.
    - **Flavor Impact:** Creates a homogenous sweet-salty base. The heat begins to cook the raw aromatics, mellowing their sharpness.
- **Step 3: Prepare Slurry**
    - **Technique:** Creating a `[[Slurry]]`.
    - **Purpose:** To suspend the cornflour starches in cold liquid *before* adding to the hot sauce. This prevents the starch from clumping.
    - **Flavor Impact:** None. This is a technical step for texture.
- **Step 4: Thicken Sauce**
    - **Technique:** `[[Starch Gelatinization]]`.
    - **Purpose:** Whisking the slurry into the hot liquid causes the starch granules to swell and burst, rapidly thickening the sauce.
    - **Flavor Impact:** Creates the characteristic glossy, viscous texture of a `[[Glaze]]`.
- **Step 5-6: Finish with Vinegar & Adjust**
    - **Technique:** `[[Balancing]]` / Finishing.
    - **Purpose:** Adding the vinegar off the heat preserves its bright acidity. Adjusting consistency ensures the sauce is usable.
    - **Flavor Impact:** The vinegar adds a final, bright acidic note that lifts the heavy sweet/salty flavors, making the sauce taste more complex.
- **Step 7-8: Store & Serve**
    - **Technique:** `[[Storage]]` and `[[Garnishing]]`.
    - **Purpose:** To cool the sauce for safe storage and to add final textural/flavor contrast at the moment of serving.
    - **Flavor Impact:** The optional garnishes add fresh (spring onion) and nutty (sesame) elements that contrast the cooked sauce.

# Flavor Synthesis

- **Primary Tastes:** The sauce is a powerful balance of **salty** (`[[Umami]]` from soy sauce) and **sweet** (from brown sugar).
- **Balance:** The **acidity** from the rice wine vinegar is crucial; it cuts through the syrupy sweetness and prevents the sauce from tasting flat or cloying.
- **Aroma:** Strong aromatics come from the infused ginger and garlic.
- **Mouthfeel:** The cornflour `[[Slurry]]` creates a thick, glossy, and viscous texture designed to cling to food.
