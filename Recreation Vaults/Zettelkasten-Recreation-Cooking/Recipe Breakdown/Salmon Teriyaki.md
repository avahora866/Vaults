# Process Analysis
- **Step 1: Cook Rice**
    - **Technique:** [[Absorption Method]].
    - **Purpose:** To hydrate and gelatinize the starch in the basmati rice, creating a fluffy texture.
    - **flavour Impact:** Creates a distinct, nutty, aromatic base that supports the strong sauce.
- **Step 2: Cook Salmon and Cut into Pieces**
    - **Technique:** [[Pan-Searing]] (likely).
    - **Purpose:** To cook the protein and create flavour via the [[Mallard Reaction]].
    - **flavour Impact:** Develops savoury, roasted notes on the exterior.
- **Step 3: Cut and Cook Vegetables**
    - **Technique:** [[Sautéing]] (for carrots/peppers); [[Slicing]] (for cucumber/onion).
    - **Purpose:** To cook the carrots and bell peppers to crisp-tender. To prepare the cucumber and spring onion as raw garnishes.
    - **flavour Impact:** Sweetens the cooked vegetables. The raw garnishes will provide a sharp, cooling, crisp contrast.
- **Step 4: Make Teriyaki Sauce (References Recipe)**
    - **Technique:** [[Reduction]] / [[Simmering]] (as a separate step).
    - **Purpose:** To dissolve sugar and reduce liquids (soy sauce, mirin, etc.) into a thick glaze.
    - **flavour Impact:** Concentrates the salty, sweet, and umami flavours.
- **Step 5: Add Vegetables and Salmon to Sauce**
    - **Technique:** [[Glazing]] / Tossing.
    - **Purpose:** To coat the *cooked* components (salmon, carrots, bell peppers) with the prepared sauce.
    - **flavour Impact:** Evenly distributes the primary teriyaki flavour onto the main ingredients.
- **Step 6: Add Everything to Rice**
    - **Technique:** Assembling.
    - **Purpose:** To compose the final dish, layering the warm, glazed items and the fresh, raw garnishes.
    - **flavour Impact:** The sauce flavours the rice. The final dish combines warm, savoury-sweet items with cool, crisp garnishes.

# flavour Synthesis
- **Primary Tastes:** The dish is driven by the **savoury** ([[Umami]] from soy sauce and salmon) and **sweet** (from the sauce and cooked vegetables) flavours.
- **Balance:** The richness of the fatty salmon and the salty-sweet sauce is balanced by the fresh, crisp cucumber and sharp spring onion. The aromatic basmati rice provides a neutral-but-fragrant base.
- **Aroma:** Strong aromatic notes from the reduced teriyaki sauce, plus the distinct nutty fragrance of the basmati rice.
- **Mouthfeel:** A combination of textures: fluffy rice, flaky salmon, crisp-tender (carrots/peppers), crisp-raw (cucumber/onion), and a thick, coating sauce.
