# Béchamel (White Sauce)

- Base Ingredients: Milk, butter, flour.
- Description: A smooth, creamy sauce made by thickening milk with a roux (butter and flour).
- Common Uses: Lasagna, macaroni and cheese, gratins, and creamy dishes.
- Derivative Sauces: Mornay (béchamel with cheese), mustard sauce, and cheddar sauce.

---

# Velouté (Light Stock Sauce)

- Base Ingredients: White stock (chicken, fish, or veal), butter, flour.
- Description: A light, silky sauce made by thickening white stock with a blonde roux.
- Common Uses: Poultry, seafood, and vegetable dishes.
- Derivative Sauces: Allemande (with egg yolks and cream), suprême (with cream and butter), and white wine sauce.

---

# Espagnole (Brown Sauce)

- Base Ingredients: Brown stock (beef or veal), butter, flour, tomatoes, mirepoix (onion, carrot, celery).
- Description: A deep, rich brown sauce thickened with a brown roux and simmered with tomatoes and aromatics.
- Common Uses: Beef and veal dishes, stews, and roasts.
- Derivative Sauces: Bordelaise (with red wine and shallots), demi-glace (reduced espagnole and stock), and chasseur (with mushrooms and wine).

---

# Tomato Sauce (Red Sauce)

- Base Ingredients: Tomatoes, olive oil or butter, onions, garlic, and sometimes stock.
- Description: A thick, tangy sauce made primarily from tomatoes, often simmered with aromatics and herbs.
- Common Uses: Pasta, pizza, meats, and seafood.
- Derivative Sauces: Marinara (simpler tomato sauce), puttanesca (with olives and capers), and creole sauce (with bell peppers and spices).

---

# Hollandaise (Egg-Emulsified Sauce)

- Base Ingredients: Egg yolks, butter, lemon juice (or vinegar), salt.
- Description: A rich, buttery sauce made by emulsifying egg yolks with melted butter and acid.
- Common Uses: Eggs Benedict, asparagus, fish, and steak.
- Derivative Sauces: Béarnaise (with tarragon and shallots), mousseline (with whipped cream), and maltaise (with blood orange juice).

# Other Basic Sauces
## Aioli

 Base Ingredients: Garlic, egg yolks, olive oil, lemon juice.  
 Description: A Mediterranean-style sauce, often used as a dip or accompaniment, made by emulsifying garlic with oil and egg yolk.  
 Common Uses: Served with grilled meats, seafood, vegetables, and fries.

## Pesto

 Base Ingredients: Fresh basil, garlic, pine nuts, Parmesan cheese, olive oil.  
 Description: A fragrant, fresh herb-based sauce made by grinding basil, garlic, and nuts with olive oil and cheese.  
 Common Uses: Pasta, sandwiches, meats, and vegetables.

## Salsa

 Base Ingredients: Tomatoes, onion, cilantro, lime, chili.  
 Description: A fresh, often chunky sauce made from raw ingredients, typically used in Mexican and Latin American dishes.  
 Common Uses: Tacos, nachos, grilled meats, and chips.

## Gravy

 Base Ingredients: Meat drippings (usually from roasted meat), flour, stock.  
 Description: A rich sauce made from meat drippings and thickened with flour.  
 Common Uses: Served with roasted meats, mashed potatoes, and dishes like turkey or chicken.

## Mustard Sauce

 Base Ingredients: Mustard (either Dijon or yellow), vinegar, cream, and sometimes stock.  
 Description: A tangy, creamy sauce made with mustard and often paired with meats like pork or chicken.  
 Common Uses: Pork, sausages, chicken, or as a dip for fries.
