Anyone can use magic but some people have more or less affinity to elements.  
Although there are people with affinity to the basic elements there are also people who are automatically able to mix elements to created specialised subclasses. The prevailing theory is that these specialised subclass are like any other magic and are not created from mixing elements instead the magic is what the person has the most affinity to. (Elemental magic and suclass magic is held in the same group).
