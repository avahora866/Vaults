# The Source and Structure

* **The Mana Organ:** A biological organ that filters, refines, and circulates ambient mana. It is the physical bottleneck for power.  
  * **Artificial Organs:** (Like the Heir's) are weaker, causing a lower **Safety Threshold** and physical weakness.  
* **Affinity:** A natural inclination towards an element (Shadow, Metal, Fire, etc.).  
* **The Paths:**  
  * **Warrior (Internal):** Mana reinforces the body for martial techniques and durability.  
  * **Mage (External):** Mana is projected outward to affect the environment.

# The Tier System: Numerical Circles

Power is defined by the complexity and density of the mana structure a user can shape. This requires **Knowledge** (understanding the Circles) and **Control** (the physical adaptation of the organ).

| Tier (Circle) | Typical User | Capability |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **1st \- 2nd Circle** | Novice/Infantry | Basic reinforcement (Metal Skin) or simple projectiles (Shadow Mist). |
| **3rd \- 4th Circle** | Adept/Commander | Complex techniques (**The Shroud**, sustained barriers). Most experienced soldiers max out here. |
| **5th \- 6th Circle** | Magister/Elite | Large-scale manipulation. The realm of generals and high-ranking sorcerers. |
| **7th+ Circle** | Grandmaster | Capable of genuinely altering the laws of physics or warfare. (Extremely rare). |

# The Limits and Curses

## Crystallization (The Overclock Mechanic)

Crystallization is not caused by stamina; it is caused by **pushing high-density mana through low-density veins**.

* **Trigger:** When a user attempts a spell/technique two or more Circles above their current mastery level (e.g., a Circle 1 user casts a Circle 3 spell).  
* **Consequence:** The strained mana pathways instantly calcify, turning the tissue into hardened, dead material to contain the energy. This is permanent.  
* **The MC's Scar:** The MC forced a Circle 3 stealth spell (**The Shroud**) at Circle 1\. His left arm now suffers from **Obsidian Frost** (patches of hardened black glass), marking him as someone who broke his body to survive.

## Forbidden Biomancy

* **The Gland Mutation (Black Ichor):** A serum derived by Elara that creates an *artificial* and controlled crystallization (Stone Skin) in the pilot's body, protecting them from the Exoskeleton's Life Drain.  
  * **Cost:** Severe addiction and accelerated aging/mutation.  
* **The Chimera Eye:** A sensory organ from a magical beast grafted onto a human.  
  * **Cost:** The **Soul Gaze** (seeing the true intent of others) and constant energy drain on the user's Mana Organ.

# The Shadow Affinity Evolution

The MC's magic progresses from soft to lethal, mirroring his psychological descent:

| Stage | Book Status | Power Focus | Physical Manifestation |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **The Haze** | Start of Book 1 | **Hiding/Evasion** (Circle 1\) | Gaseous, translucent smoke, used to hide presence. |
| **The Shroud** | Post-Massacre | **Survival/Stealth** (Circle 3 Overclock) | Hard, heavy cloak that dampens sound/scent. Caused the **Obsidian Frost** scar. |
| **The Needle** | End of Book 1 | **Lethality/Assassination** (Circle 3\) | Solidified, crystallized shadows used as non-traceable weapons (Umbral Spike). |
| **The Truth-Seer** | Book 2 | **Information/Inquisition** (Circle 5 Focus) | The power is now in the **Chimera Eye**. Active shadow magic is mostly abandoned due to mana drainage and fear of crystallization. |
