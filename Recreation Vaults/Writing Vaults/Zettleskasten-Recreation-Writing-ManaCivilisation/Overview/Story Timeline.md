World Bible: The Two Wars Duology Timeline

This timeline is split into two distinct, high-stakes narrative arcs, each concluding with a massive military/political climax.

Book 1: The Fortress of Crows (The War of Faith)

Theme: Survival requires sacrifice. To save the family, the Old Ways must die. Focus: The rise of the Iron Guard, the Heir's obsession with efficiency, and the MC's descent into shadow work.

| Phase | Event | Characters Involved | Consequence |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **I. The Inciting Incident** | The family joins the Civil War. The Heir is assigned to Logistics; the MC is assigned to Scouts. | Heir, MC, Father | The Heir feels useless (due to the **Pitying Look**). The MC is exposed to the trauma of the **Scout Massacre** and is physically scarred by **Obsidian Frost**. |
| **II. The Invention** | The Heir discovers the **Ghost Calories** logistical puzzle. He meets **Kaelen** (the Engineer) and begins developing the **Exoskeleton Infantry** to overcome his own weakness. | Heir, Kaelen, MC | The MC begins his double agent work for **Varrick** (Merchant) and **Vane** (Spymaster) to fund the secret project. |
| **III. The Crisis** | The Father is convinced by the Church to lead a suicide mission against the **Fortress of Crows** to achieve "absolution" for the family's sins. | Father, Heir, MC, Inquisitor Silas | The Heir makes the desperate decision to sacrifice the **Penitent Veterans** using the Exoskeletons to save his father. |
| **IV. The Climax (Siege)** | The **Vertical Flank** fails to save the Father. He dies on the bridge, seeing the heresy (the Exoskeletons) that almost saved him. The brothers kill the enemy Sorcerer-Prince. | Heir, MC, Father (Death) | **The Tragedy:** The Heir is consumed by hatred for the Church. The brothers lie, claiming the victory was a miracle (The Martyrdom Lie), protecting the family but cementing their guilt. |


Book 2: The Red Haze (The War of Lies)

Theme: Victory is a cage. To keep power, you must become the monster you fear. Focus: The Heir's political betrayal, the MC's psychological collapse, and the creation of monstrous new power.

| Phase | Event | Characters Involved | Consequence |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **I. The Golden Cage** | The Heir is confirmed as the new Count. He marries **Lady Elara** (The Biomancer) for political protection and resources. | Heir, Elara, MC | Elara discovers the Exoskeleton **Life Drain** and the Heir's artificial organ. She blackmails him for funding for her **Chimera** experiments. |
| **II. The Betrayal** | **Varrick** (The Survivor) leaks the Fortress's weaknesses to the **Administrator Prince**. Elite assassins with **Null Cloaks** attack the Heir. | MC, Heir, Varrick, Silencers | The MC's Shadow Sense fails. The Heir is saved by Elara's Chimera, giving the Administrator Prince proof of heresy (The Blackmail). |
| **III. The Transformation** | The Administrator Prince demands the **Defective Batch**. The Heir agrees, creating the **"Red Haze" Protocol**. The MC, desperate to see the invisible, takes the **Chimera Eye** from Elara. | Heir, MC, Elara, Kaelen | The Heir creates the **Stone-Skin Addiction** (Black Ichor). The MC gains the **Soul Gaze**, seeing the true, dark intentions of everyone, including his brother. |
| **IV. The Climax (Capital Siege)** | The Military Prince (the Heir's patron) charges the capital. The **"Red Haze" Protocol** activates. The Exoskeletons perform a **Friendly Fire** massacre. | Heir, MC, Admin Prince, Military Prince (Death) | **The Triumph:** The Admin Prince wins the war. The Heir is rewarded with a Dukedom and the Royal Foundry. The MC is made the King's Inquisitor. They are rich, powerful, and utterly corrupted. |
