# Character Arc
The Shadow Who Saw Too Much
The MC’s arc is a psychological horror story wrapped in a spy thriller. While the Heir loses his morality to ambition/vengeance, the MC loses his sanity to truth. He starts as the brother who protects the family from the dark, and ends as a man who knows that the family is the dark.

Book 1: The Death of Innocence
1. The Starting State (The Shield)

The Mindset: He is content being the "Spare." He doesn't resent the Heir; actually, he is protective of him. He knows his brother is physically weak (due to the organ), so the MC views himself as the physical shield.

The Skill: His "Shadow Affinity" is undeveloped. He uses it for hide-and-seek or minor pranks. It’s innocent.

2. The Trauma (The Scout Massacre)

The Incident: This is the defining moment. The Noble Officer sends the unit into a trap. The unit is slaughtered.

The Survival: The MC survives not by fighting, but by hiding under the corpses of his friends. He lies in the mud for days, breathing in the scent of blood, using his shadow magic to stay invisible while enemy soldiers walk past.

The Lesson: Honor gets you killed. Invisibility keeps you alive. He returns to camp changed—quieter, colder, and realizing that his Father’s "Honor" is a lie that kills good men.

3. The Descent (The Cleaner)

The Shift: He joins the Military Police to hunt the traitor Officer. When he finds him, he realizes the law won't punish a noble. So, he plants the evidence (framing him).

The Burden: When the Merchant Varrick blackmails him, the MC doesn't tell the Heir. He decides to "eat the sin" himself. He starts smuggling, stealing, and killing to keep his brother’s hands clean. He believes the Heir is the "White Knight" of the family who must remain pure.

4. The Climax (The Fortress of Crows)

The Role: While the Heir climbs the cliff, the MC infiltrates the fortress. He kills the guards in the dark.

The Turning Point: After the battle, when the Heir decides to lie about the Father's death ("The Martyrdom"), the MC supports it. He thinks, "My brother is finally playing the game. We are partners." He doesn't realize he just enabled a monster.

Book 2: The Death of Trust
1. The Crisis (The Blindness)

The Threat: Varrick returns with the Null Cloak Assassins.

The Failure: The MC is the master of shadows, but he cannot see these enemies. They walk right past him and nearly kill the Heir.

The Fear: For the first time since the Scout Massacre, he feels exposed. He is "under the corpses" again, helpless. He decides he will never be blind again, no matter the cost.

2. The Sacrifice (The Eye)

The Bargain: He goes to Lady Elara. He knows she is dangerous, but he needs to see the invisible. He offers his own eye.

The Surgery: It is brutal and traumatizing, cementing his distrust of Elara (who enjoys it too much).

3. The Curse (The First Look)

The Tragedy: The bandages come off. He looks at his brother, expecting to see the "White Knight" he protected all these years.

The Vision: Through the Soul Gaze, he sees the Heir's aura. It isn't white. It is a swirling, suffocating Black and Grey. He sees that the Heir views the MC not as a brother, but as a "useful tool."

The Realization: The MC realizes he sacrificed his humanity to protect a man who would sacrifice him without hesitation. The love dies in that room, replaced by terror.

4. The Ending State (The Golden Cage)

The Role: He becomes the King's Inquisitor. He wears the eyepatch not to hide the eye, but to dampen the visions.

The Coping Mechanism: He drinks heavily or uses drugs to blur the colors of the Soul Gaze. He serves the Heir (now Duke) not out of loyalty, but because he knows everyone else (The King, Elara) is even worse. He is trapped in a world of monsters that only he can see.


## Magic
The MC’s Shadow Progression (Revised)
The MC’s arc is now physically marked by that one night under the corpses.

1. The Trauma Event (The Scout Massacre)
Situation: The MC is a Circle 1 (Novice). He can only make shadows vague and misty.

The Necessity: Enemy elite soldiers (Circle 3 or 4) are walking through the camp. If the MC uses his weak mist, they will see him. He needs "The Shroud" (a Circle 3 Technique that completely masks sound, scent, and sight).

The Sacrifice: He forces the technique. He holds it for hours while lying under heavy bodies.

The Result: He survives, but the mana overload crystallizes a specific part of his body.

2. The Scar: "Obsidian Frost"
Since he has a Shadow Affinity, his crystallization doesn't look like grey rock.

Visual: It looks like semi-transparent black glass or frozen smoke (Obsidian).

Location: likely his left arm/hand (the one clutching his mouth or weapon) or a patch on his chest/neck.

Effect: That body part is cold, hard, and numb. It has reduced mobility.

Social Impact: In Book 1, he wears gloves or high collars not just for style, but to hide the proof that he performed forbidden/high-level magic. It marks him as someone who "survived the impossible."

3. The Evolution (Book 1 & 2)
Book 1: The "Cleaner" Phase

Rank: He is technically still Circle 1 or 2 physically, but he has the knowledge of Circle 3.

Tactics: Because using high-level magic hurts him/increases the crystal, he relies heavily on low-mana efficiency and mundane kills (knives/poison). He only uses the "Umbral Spike" (High Tier) as a finisher because it risks spreading the Obsidian Frost further up his arm.

Book 2: The Chimera Eye

The Eye: As you noted, this does not cause crystallization.

The Trade-off: The Eye drains his Fuel (Mana reserves).

The Danger: If the Eye drains his mana tank to near-zero, his "Safety Threshold" drops. If he tries to cast even a simple spell while empty, his body might interpret it as "Overclocking," risking more crystallization.

Combat Style: He becomes a "Gun-Mage." He uses the Eye to aim (Soul Gaze/Thermal) and mundane pistols/crossbows to kill. He almost never uses offensive shadow magic anymore because his body is too fragile/scarred from the past, and the Eye takes all his spare energy.

Summary of the MC's Magic Arc
Novice: Uses harmless smoke.

The Overclock (Massacre): Forces a Master-tier stealth spell ("Shroud"). Survives, but his left arm turns to Obsidian Glass.

The Efficient Killer: Uses minimum magic to avoid spreading the glass. Relies on stealth and knives.

The Truth-Seer: Gains the Chimera Eye. It drains his battery constantly. He stops using active magic almost entirely, relying on the Eye for intel and his network/guns for violence.

## Obsedien arm
That is locked in. The Obsidian Arm becomes the defining physical trait of the MC. It’s a constant reminder: He survived, but he broke himself to do it. It also adds tension to every fight—if he pushes too hard again, the glass will spread to his heart.
