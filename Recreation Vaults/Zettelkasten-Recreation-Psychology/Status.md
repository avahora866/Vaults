
### Social Psychology

* **Conformity & Obedience**

  * *Topic*: How people change behavior to fit in or follow authority.
  * *Experiment/Observation*: Read about the Asch conformity experiment or Milgram’s obedience study; notice moments in daily life where people follow trends or authority.
  * *Why it matters*: Explains peer pressure, workplace behavior, and social influence.

* **Persuasion & Influence**

  * *Topic*: How opinions and choices are shaped.
  * *Experiment*: Analyze ads or political speeches; identify tactics like reciprocity, social proof, or authority.
  * *Why*: Helps understand marketing, leadership, and negotiation.

* **Group Dynamics**

  * *Topic*: Behavior in groups vs alone.
  * *Observation*: Notice how people act in teams or crowds; think about roles people take, decision-making, and diffusion of responsibility.
  * *Why*: Explains teamwork, mob behavior, and social responsibility.

### Cognitive Psychology

* **Decision-Making & Biases**

  * *Topic*: How people make choices, often irrationally.
  * *Experiment*: Look into heuristics like anchoring, availability bias, or loss aversion. Track your own decisions for examples.
  * *Why*: Explains financial choices, risk-taking, and daily judgments.

* **Memory & Perception**

  * *Topic*: How we interpret and remember events.
  * *Experiment*: Try a simple memory test—remember a list of items, notice what’s easier or harder.
  * *Why*: Shows why eyewitnesses can be unreliable and how expectations shape perception.

* **Problem-Solving & Creativity**

  * *Topic*: How we approach challenges.
  * *Experiment*: Solve puzzles in multiple ways; notice mental blocks and strategies.
  * *Why*: Explains innovation, habits, and learning strategies.

### Behavioral Psychology

* **Conditioning & Habits**

  * *Topic*: How actions are learned and maintained.
  * *Experiment*: Observe your habits; try a small reward/punishment change and note the effect.
  * *Why*: Explains habits, addiction, and skill acquisition.

* **Motivation & Reinforcement**

  * *Topic*: Why we pursue certain actions.
  * *Observation*: Track what drives your choices (intrinsic vs. extrinsic motivation).
  * *Why*: Helps understand goals, productivity, and behavior change.

### Personality Psychology

* **Traits & Individual Differences**

  * *Topic*: Why people respond differently to the same situation.
  * *Experiment*: Take a personality test (like Big Five) and reflect on its accuracy.
  * *Why*: Explains leadership, friendships, and career choices.

* **Temperament & Coping**

  * *Topic*: How inherent traits affect stress, emotion, and decisions.
  * *Observation*: Notice reactions to stress in yourself and others.
  * *Why*: Explains resilience, emotional regulation, and social interactions.

### Optional Extensions

* **Behavioral Economics**: Study irrational choices in money, time, and effort.
* **Abnormal Psychology**: Understand extreme behaviors and mental disorders to see patterns of deviation from the norm.
* **Neuropsychology**: Learn how brain function shapes behavior—especially motivation, reward, and impulse control.
