# Mothers Side
- Nana and Nani = Mothers parents
- Mama = Mothers brother
	- Mami = Mothers brothers wife
- Masi = Mothers sister
	- Masa = Mothers sisters wife 

# Dads Side
- Dada and Dadi = Fathers parents
- Tau = Fathers elder brother
	- Tajii = Fathers elder brothers wife
- Chacha/Kaka = Fathers younger brother
	- Chachi/Kaki = Fathers younger brothers wife
- Foi = Fathers sister
	- Phuba = Fathers sisters husband
