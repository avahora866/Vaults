Conversation fillers, like "um," "uh," "like," "you know," and "I mean," serve several functions in speech. They help:

1. **Buy Time** – They give the speaker a moment to think while keeping control of the conversation.
2. **Signal Hesitation or Uncertainty** – They indicate that the speaker is unsure or searching for the right words.
3. **Maintain Flow** – They prevent awkward silences and keep the conversation going.
4. **Soften Statements** – They make statements sound less direct or blunt, which can help with politeness or social comfort.
5. **Indicate Familiarity or Casualness** – They make speech sound more natural and conversational, especially in informal settings.

>Though often seen as unnecessary, fillers can make speech feel more human and relatable. However, overusing them might make someone seem unsure or unprepared.
