# Conversation Idea Generator Framework

## 1. **FORCE** Topics
This is an acronym you can keep in mind to cover a wide range of casual conversation topics:
- **F**amily / Friends  
- **O**ccupation / school / what they’re working on  
- **R**ecreation (hobbies, movies, games, music, travel)  
- **C**urrent events / pop culture  
- **E**nvironment (the setting you're both in)

These are go-tos that work in almost any situation.

## 2. **Observation + Curiosity**
Comment on something in the moment, then ask something about it:
- “That’s a cool mug—where’d you get it?”  
- “You seem super focused—working on something intense?”  
- “This playlist is a vibe—do you know who made it?”

The formula is:
**[Make an Observation] + [Ask a Follow-up Question]**

## 3. **Use "Three What's"**
If someone says something, mentally run:
- **What** does that mean?
- **What** does that say about them?
- **What** can I relate to or ask about from that?

Example:
Them: “I went rock climbing this weekend.”  
You: “Oh nice! What kind of climbing do you do? (what) / Is that something you do often? (what about them) / I’ve always wanted to try it. (relate)”

## 4. **Have “Evergreen” Questions Ready**
These are timeless and can spark deeper convo:
- “What’s something you’ve been into lately?”
- “What would you do if you had a totally free weekend?”
- “What’s the last thing you got nerdy about?”

## 5. **Flip the Spotlight**
If you feel stuck, flip the focus:
- “What do *you* think about that?”
- “I’m curious—how do you usually handle stuff like that?”

## 6. **Practice Free Association**
Practice riffing off a single word. If someone says "coffee," what does that bring to mind? Café culture, productivity, morning rituals, funny caffeine stories?  
Jot down a word and just brainstorm 5 questions or thoughts you could spin from it. Do it daily as a mental warmup.
