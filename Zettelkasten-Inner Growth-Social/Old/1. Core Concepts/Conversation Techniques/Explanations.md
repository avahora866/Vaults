## How to Choose the Right Framework:

- ### Best Option for Coding Problems:
	- Use a Hybrid Approach: Combine [[Explanations#The 5W1H Framework|5W1H]] for context and [[Explanations#The MECE Principle (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive)|MECE]] for organization.
	    - Start with What, Where, and When for context.
	    - Use Symptoms, Causes, Steps Taken, and Next Steps to structure your explanation.

### The Feynman Technique

1. Choose a concept and study it.
2. Explain it in simple terms as if teaching someone with no prior knowledge.
3. Identify gaps in your understanding and revisit the material.
4. Simplify and refine your explanation.

### The Explain Like I’m Five (ELI5) Method

- Break the topic down into its simplest components.
- Use analogies, metaphors, and simple language.
- Focus on how things work step by step.


### The Pyramid Principle

- Start with the main idea or conclusion.
- Break it into key supporting points.
- Provide detailed evidence or examples under each point.


###  The 5W1H Framework

- Explain the What, Why, Who, Where, When, and How of the topic.
- Useful for giving a comprehensive overview of a subject.

### The STAR Framework (Situation, Task, Action, Result)

- Commonly used in storytelling or sharing experiences.
- Helps structure explanations about processes or events.


### The PEEL Framework (Point, Evidence, Explanation, Link)

Useful for making arguments or presenting points:

- Point: State your main idea.
- Evidence: Provide supporting information.
- Explanation: Clarify the evidence.
- Link: Connect back to the main idea or broader context.


### The MECE Principle (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive)

- Divide information into distinct categories without overlap.
- Ensure the categories cover all aspects of the topic.


###  SOCO (Single Overriding Communication Objective)

- Focus on the most important takeaway message.
- Use supporting points to reinforce the core idea.


### The 3-Act Storytelling Framework

- Act 1: Set the stage and introduce the context.
- Act 2: Explore the conflict or challenge.
- Act 3: Resolve the challenge and conclude.
