# Handling people
- Don't **criticize, condemn, or complain**
- Give honest and sincere **appreciation**
- Arouse in the other person an eager want (**Importance**)
# Convincing People

- Avoid **argument** ("The only way to get the best of an argument is to avoid it")
- Respect **opinions** ("Show respect for the other person’s opinions. Never say 'You're wrong'")
- Emphasize **admitting wrongs** ("If you are wrong, admit it quickly and emphatically")
- **Friendly** approach ("Begin in a friendly way")
- Get '**yes**' immediately ("Get the other person saying 'yes, yes' immediately")
- Let them **talk** ("Let the other person do a great deal of the talking")
- **His or her idea** ("Let the other person feel that the idea is his or hers")
- Look from **their perspective** ("Try honestly to see things from the other person's point of view")
- Feel **sympathy** ("Be sympathetic with the other person's ideas and desires")
- Talk to **nobler motives** ("Appeal to the nobler motives")
- Show ideas **dramatically** ("Dramatize your ideas")
- Give a **challenge** ("Throw down a challenge")

# Making People Like You
- Become **genuinely interested** in other people
- **Smile**
- Remember their **name** ("A person’s name is the sweetest sound in any language")
- Be a **good listener** ("Encourage others to talk about themselves")
- Talk in terms of their **interests**
- Make them **feel important** ("And do it sincerely")
# Changing People
- **Begin** with **praise** and **honest appreciation**
- Call attention to people's mistakes **indirectly**
- Talk about your **own mistakes** before criticizing the other person
- **Ask questions** instead of giving direct orders
- Let the other person **save face**
- Praise the **slightest improvement** and praise every improvement. Be hearty in your appreciation and lavish in your praise
- Give the other person a **fine reputation** to live up to
- Use encouragement. Make the **fault seem easy to correct**
- Make the other person **happy about doing the thing you suggest**
