When I get uncomfortable during a conversation my brain doesn't supply me with the information it usually does (or it does but my feeling of pressure and being uncomfortable makes me ignore it). 
To combat this when I realise I am uncomfortable:
- take a pause (1-3 sec) whilst showing acknowledgement (nod or smile)
- If my heart is beating faster try breathing in deeply to slow it down
- When I am unsure how to reply use default phrases like:
	- “That’s an interesting thought. What do you think about…?”
	- “Hmm, I’m not sure about that—tell me more.”
	to help give you a few seconds. Also try to redirect the conversation back to the recipient to allow you to compose yourself.

The above are what to do in the moment (in the conversation) to help the conversation and move it forward however to combat this long term their are strategies I can use to be better at communicating.
