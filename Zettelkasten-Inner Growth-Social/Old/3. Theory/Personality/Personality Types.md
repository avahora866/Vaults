Psychologists and researchers use several different systems to group and measure human personality. The most prominent models operate on either trait spectrums or distinct categorical types.

# Big Five

Psychologists consider the Big Five the most scientifically accurate model. It measures your personality across five spectrums rather than assigning a strict type.

| Trait | High Score | Low Score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Openness | Curious, creative, prefers variety | Predictable, prefers routine |
| Conscientiousness | Organized, disciplined, careful | Careless, impulsive, disorganized |
| Extraversion | Sociable, outgoing, talkative | Reserved, prefers solitude |
| Agreeableness | Cooperative, trusting, kind | Critical, skeptical, hostile |
| Neuroticism | Anxious, volatile, stressed | Calm, stable, resilient |

# Myers-Briggs Type Indicator

This commercial framework assigns you one of 16 types based on your cognitive preferences. It groups these types into four main categories.

| Category | Types | Primary Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Analysts | INTJ, INTP, ENTJ, ENTP | Logic, strategy, problem-solving |
| Diplomats | INFJ, INFP, ENFJ, ENFP | Empathy, ideals, human connection |
| Sentinels | ISTJ, ISFJ, ESTJ, ESFJ | Order, rules, practical application |
| Explorers | ISTP, ISFP, ESTP, ESFP | Spontaneity, action, adaptability |

# Enneagram

This system categorizes you based on your core fears and motivations. It divides nine personality types into three centers of intelligence.

| Center | Driving Emotion | Types |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gut | Anger | 8 (Challenger), 9 (Peacemaker), 1 (Reformer) |
| Heart | Shame | 2 (Helper), 3 (Achiever), 4 (Individualist) |
| Head | Fear | 5 (Investigator), 6 (Loyalist), 7 (Enthusiast) |

# DISC Assessment

Corporate environments frequently use this behavioral tool. It assesses how you lead, work in teams, and communicate.

| Style | Focus | Characteristics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dominance (D) | Results | Direct, firm, driven, competitive |
| Influence (I) | People | Enthusiastic, optimistic, sociable |
| Steadiness (S) | Stability | Patient, accommodating, tactful |
| Conscientiousness (C) | Accuracy | Analytical, reserved, precise |
