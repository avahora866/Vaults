Entitlement can be a useful tool for getting ahead in the modern world. You can’t get what you want unless you first believe you deserve it.

Background matters because it shapes the opportunities available to you. It’s not just about connections for jobs—it's also about growing up with access to books, supportive parenting, and the kind of upbringing that teaches entitlement as a norm.

These factors contribute to success. In contrast, a difficult childhood doesn’t offer the same advantages—it doesn’t make you more prepared just because you struggled.
