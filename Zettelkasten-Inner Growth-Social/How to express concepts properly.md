# Problem
First my problem with expressing concepts is that I stumble over my words, I have no clear direction on where I am going so it comes out as an incoherent mess. I find I have something I want to say I start to say it, I do remember to give context on the situation but after that I don't know how to keep the conversation flowing after presenting my point. Then I start to stumble over my words trying to make the conversation flow.

The above is for when I am talking about various concepts unrelated to me, but when I am talking about myself I stumble almost immediately.
# What's happening
## Low Stake conversations
Incoherence in low stakes conversations occur when you do not lead with the interesting point. For example instead of starting with the context of the situation start with the interesting point:
- _"So I was reading about this thing, and it reminded me of what we discussed, and there's this concept I've been thinking about..."_ → nobody knows where this is going
- _"Do you think expertise makes you worse at explaining things?"_ → now they're engaged, and you can add context if needed

Everything else is just adding form to the interesting point

## Emotional Conversations
Emotional conversations come with anxiety and various other emotions which speed up your words making them even faster then your thoughts, thus stumbling over them.
During emotional conversations remember to take a deep breath to help reset yourself, and take some time to think through your responses:
You can always say I need some time to think about it so I have a proper response.
Never say something you are not sure of like agreeing with something you are unsure.
